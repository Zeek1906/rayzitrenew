How It WorksWhat RAYZIT Renew Is

RAYZIT Renew prepares your organization to receive charitable contributions from donor organizations such as Amazon that cannot sell certain items under their compliance policies because of minor packaging damage. With the RAYZIT Renew program, we will teach you step by step how to build real relationships with donor partners and pair those contributions with the RAYZIT app to grow your own revenue.

The RAYZIT Renew program has helped organizations such as the HEALS Network, Inc., where they routinely receive cycles of surplus goods valued on average of around $90K, helping them serve approximately 450 families.

These goods are available subject to the following conditions:

  • 1The rate at which manufacturers identify and collect products that do not meet their standard distribution requirements.
  • 2The number of organizations in your area participating in the RAYZIT Renew program.

The products available through this program are not predetermined. They depend entirely on which items are damaged during the manufacturer's operations at any given time.

ReadinessBe Ready To Receive

Donor partners need confidence that items reach the community quickly and are accounted for. Thus, below are the following expectations:

  • 1Pickup: a vehicle or volunteer team able to collect within the donor's window, usually 24 to 72 hours.
  • 2Storage: clean, dry, secure space sized for the donations you request; cold storage if accepting food.
  • 3Distribution plan: where, when and to whom items go, including partner sites or events.
  • 4Volunteers: enough people to sort, load and distribute on each pickup day.
  • 5Tracking: record items received, families or people served, and estimated value.
  • 6No resale: donated items go to the community at no cost and are never resold.
  • 7Reporting: send an impact report with photos within 30 days of each contribution.

WhyWhy Complement Your RAYZIT Renew Campaign with the RAYZIT App?

RAYZIT Renew connects organizations with a supply of goods. The RAYZIT App turns the goodwill that supply creates into recurring revenue. Here's why:

  • 1Distribution events draw crowds, producing potential new donors.
  • 2Volunteers become supporters, more willing to give now that they have a personal stake in the mission.
  • 3Someone moved by a distribution event can scan a code and start giving spare change on the spot.
  • 4Families helped by the organization often want to give back in a small, accessible way.
  • 5Round-up giving happens automatically, so a one-time moment of goodwill becomes continuous support.
  • 6RAYZIT Renew is supply. The RAYZIT App is sustainability.
  • 7Without the RAYZIT App, organizations depend on grants and one-off fundraising to support their mission.
